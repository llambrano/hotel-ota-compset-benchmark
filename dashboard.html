    How to read this dashboard

    An OTA's monthly production extract gives each hotel's own results but reports its compset only as year-over-year growth (and as level gaps for share metrics). So every comparison here is hotel growth vs compset growth: if the hotel grew room nights 8% and its compset grew 3%, the gap is +5 points and the hotel is gaining share of the channel.

    Scorecard

    • Hotel YoY / Compset YoY: the month's year-over-year change for the hotel and for its compset.
    • Gap: hotel YoY minus compset YoY, in percentage points. Blue above +1, orange below −1, gray in between.
    • 12-month gap: the gap in each of the last 12 months, oldest to newest.
    • Months ahead: how many of those months the gap was above +1 pt.
    • Sponsored Listings: bookings from paid placements as a share of the hotel's OTA bookings. Here the gap is the hotel's share minus the compset's share, in points.

    Hotel detail

    • The top panel is the hotel's monthly value (room nights, revenue, ADR or sponsored share).
    • The middle panel shows the two growth lines: hotel YoY (solid) and compset YoY (dashed).
    • The gap strip underneath is the difference between them, month by month.
    • The Mix tab covers the other compset comparisons: package, mobile, international, cancellation, refundable and Visibility Boost share (in points), plus length of stay and booking window (in days).

    Things to know

    • A hotel that opened during the window has no prior year, so it has no YoY and no gap until it laps its opening.
    • Very large YoY swings usually mean a small base. Check the monthly values before reading too much into them.
